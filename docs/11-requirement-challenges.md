# 12. Challenges to the stated requirements

| Your requirement | The problem | Proposed resolution |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Autonomous** + **2. HA provides the rules** | Directly in tension. Undefined: autonomous of *what* — HA, internet, or mains? | Split **authoring** (HA) from **ownership and execution** (device). HA-computed modifiers carry a TTL decaying to permissive. → FR-1, FR-2 |
| **3. Fall back to closed on any issue** | **As written this destroys requirement 1.** A Wi-Fi blip would stop the run, and Wi-Fi blips are the most common event in the system's life. Also, "fail closed" is not implementable in software alone — you can only *command* closed. And it is silent on the power-cut-mid-run question. | Split into hardware fail-closed (non-negotiable) + a per-fault-class policy table you sign off + independent over-run enforcement + a master valve and flow meter so "closed" is *observable*. → [§5](03-fault-policy.md) |
| **4. Sequential to avoid low pressure** | Cycle time is the **sum** of all zone runs — plausibly ~1 h 15 (sprays) to ~4 h 30 (rotors). Ordering, master valve, settle delay and overflow policy were all unspecified. "Always one zone" is also a crude proxy for a hydraulic limit. *(An earlier draft asserted "8 × 20 min = 2 h 40 won't fit a 05:00–07:00 window" as fact; **both** numbers were analysis assumptions.)* | Per-zone L/min budget + `max_concurrent_zones` fixed at 1; master valve with lead/lag; priority + rotation. **D4 removed the window entirely** — no overrun policy is needed; instead FR-4.6a skips a start that collides with a running cycle. → FR-4 |
| **5. Frequency per day/week + range of time** | **The most underspecified of the ten.** "Range of time" could mean a window, a duration range, or a literal on-time — three different data models. "3× per week" doesn't say which days. Multiple runs/day unresolved. And the deepest issue: minutes vs litres vs **mm of water depth** (agronomically the correct unit). **D4 deletes the ambiguity rather than resolving it: a start time + a duration, no window at all.** Composable weekday-mask / every-N-days / parity predicates; **minutes in v1** so the core loop never depends on the flow meter; litres and mm in v2/v3, always bounded by a max duration. → FR-5 |
| **6. Monitor water like the pool** | Two real traps: **cheap flow sensors cannot see drip flows at all**, and the pool sensor you'd be mirroring **is itself broken** (not reboot-persistent). Totalisation location unspecified. | Volumetric 1 L/pulse meter; device-side persisted totals; HA `utility_meter` for day/month/year; `flow_unmetered` flag for sub-floor zones with volumes clearly labelled *estimated*. → FR-6, [§10](09-water-metering.md) |
| **7. Schedule vs actual** | **You can't compare against a plan you never recorded.** And HA's recorder purges detail after ~10 days, so only `state_class` sensors survive as statistics — volumes persist, *events* don't. | Materialise the daily plan; per-run durable records with outcome + reason; both plan and actual as `state_class` sensors; JSONL archive for 10-year readability. → FR-7 |
| **8. Pause the scheduled system** | Six unanswered questions. Skip or defer? Does it stop an in-flight run? **And the failure mode nobody plans for: the forgotten indefinite pause, which is how gardens die — silently.** | Three named verbs (Hold / Pause / Stop); **skip, never queue**; mandatory duration with a 7-day cap, a warning 24 h before, then auto-resume; doesn't block manual; persisted, attributed; separate Winter/Off mode that *does* block everything. → FR-8 |
| **9. Alarm when something is wrong** | An undifferentiated boolean is the requirement most likely to be muted within a month. No severity, no acknowledgement, no false-positive strategy — and **a dead controller cannot raise its own alarm.** | Three severities, latching table, acknowledgement flow, HA availability watchdog on `unavailable`, and a **weekly heartbeat whose absence is the signal**. → FR-9, [§11](10-alarms.md) |
| **10. Manual trigger for some time** | The naive implementation ("HA turns a switch on, waits, turns it off") leaves the valve **open indefinitely** on any Wi-Fi drop or HA restart in that window — and it will be exercised dozens of times a season by a human. Preempt-vs-queue, hard cap, and schedule-credit are all unspecified. | On-device duration enforcement, hard cap, preempt-the-current-zone, `satisfied_by_manual` at ≥80 % to prevent double-watering. **Generalised into P4: every open-valve command carries its own expiry.** → FR-10 |
